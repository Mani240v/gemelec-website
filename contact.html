Good to know

Quick answers.

Do you charge for quotes?
No. Quotes for standard residential and small commercial jobs are free, whether we visit on site or scope the work from photos you send through. Larger commercial or strata scopes that need a full site survey are flagged upfront before we start, so there are never any surprises on the bill.
What's your typical response time?
Form enquiries get a reply inside two hours during business hours. Phone calls are picked up live whenever we can, and if we're on the tools we'll call you back within thirty minutes. Emergencies bypass the queue and go straight to the on-call sparky.
Do you do strata work?
Yes, regularly. We work with strata managers and building managers across the eastern suburbs and inner south on common-property switchboards, lighting, emergency exit testing, and unit-level callouts. Tax invoicing, scope documentation, and after-hours coordination are all handled in-house.
