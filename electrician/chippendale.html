The Local Mix

ECO BUILDS,
GALLERIES, EVS.

Chippendale is one of the inner city's most quietly modern postcodes. The redevelopment around Central Park brought a generation of sustainability-minded apartment buildings, and the suburb has the highest EV uptake we see on this side of town. Most weeks we're sizing cable for a new charger, retrofitting a tail into an existing meter cupboard, or troubleshooting a unit that needs a load-management upgrade before strata signs off on another spot.

Off Kensington Street, the gallery and small-bar precinct keeps us busy with commercial work. Track lighting that needs to dim properly for a hanging, three-phase for a kitchen we're sharing with the venue next door, exit signs and emergency lighting that hold up to annual certification. We do the design, the install, and the test report in one trip.

The older fabric of Chippendale is still here too. Worker's cottages, ex-industrial conversions, and the occasional terrace where the wiring hasn't been touched since the seventies. Heritage-aware installs, careful chasing, and proper rewiring without ruining the brickwork. We handle the lot, from a thousand-volt apartment switchboard to a single tired light fitting.
