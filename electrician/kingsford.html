Electrical work in Kingsford

Kingsford runs along Anzac Parade, sandwiched between the university precinct and the racecourse. It's a busy mix of student housing, family homes, walk-up apartment blocks, and a long strip of shops, restaurants, and takeaways. We work across all of it, and being next-door in Matraville means we can usually swing past quickly.

A lot of the residential work in Kingsford is on older red-brick walk-up flats and post-war family homes that have been through multiple landlords and tenants. Switchboards in these places are often a patchwork of additions, and we frequently find missing earth, undersized cabling to the kitchen, or no RCD protection at all. We sort those out cleanly with a board upgrade and proper circuit splits.

Along the Anzac Parade strip, we look after small business sites — kebab shops, cafes, hair salons, dental and beauty clinics, a few small offices above the shops. Three-phase faults, exit/emergency lighting compliance, kitchen circuits for new fit-outs, and after-hours installs are regular jobs for us in this part of the suburb. EV chargers are climbing fast among Kingsford homeowners too, especially in the streets behind the parade.
