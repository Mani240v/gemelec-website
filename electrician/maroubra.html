Electrical work in Maroubra

Maroubra sits about ten minutes from our Matraville base, which makes it one of the suburbs we get to most often. The housing mix here keeps the work varied. You've got the older red-brick semis up around Bunnerong Road, the post-war fibro homes that have been progressively renovated, and the larger strata buildings clustered closer to the beach. Each comes with its own quirks.

The big jobs we see in Maroubra fall into three buckets. First, switchboard upgrades. A lot of the original homes still run ceramic fuses or early-generation safety switches that no longer meet modern load demands once a household adds split systems, induction cooktops, or a heat pump. Second, strata work. The apartment blocks near the foreshore have ageing common-area lighting, intercom systems, and basement car park circuits that need careful, scheduled replacement. Third, salt-air corrosion. Outdoor power points, sensor lights, and pool equipment near the beach degrade faster than they do inland, and they need to be specced for the environment, not just installed and forgotten.

We also do a fair bit of EV charger work in Maroubra now. The newer townhouses and the strata garages have driven a real uptick in installs over the last twelve months, and most require a load assessment before a 7kW or 11kW charger can go in safely. If your block has a body corporate, we'll handle the paperwork side too.

Whatever you've got, we'll show up on time, quote properly, and clean up before we leave. That's the standard, every job.
