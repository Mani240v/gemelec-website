Electrical work in Randwick

Randwick is one of the busiest pockets we work in. You've got a real mix here: Federation terraces around the racecourse and the hospital precinct, mid-century walk-up flats around Avoca and Belmore Road, and newer apartment developments closer to the light rail. The variety means every Randwick job needs a different eye.

For the older homes near Coogee Bay Road and the Frenchmans Road end, we see a lot of original two-wire wiring, undersized mains, and switchboards that pre-date RCD requirements. A switchboard upgrade with proper RCD circuits is the first thing we recommend when the wiring is borderline. For strata-managed buildings around the hospital and university, we work directly with strata managers and provide compliance certificates for common-area lighting, emergency exits, and tenancy fit-outs.

There's also a steady run of light-commercial work along Belmore Road and around Randwick Junction, from cafes and dental clinics to small medical suites. Three-phase, exit/emergency lighting, dedicated circuits for medical equipment, and after-hours installs so business doesn't stop — all standard for us. EV charger installs have been climbing fast in the area too, especially in the newer townhouse complexes off Avoca Street.
