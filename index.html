Frequently Asked

Questions we get a lot.

Does Gemelec offer 24/7 emergency electrical services in Sydney?

Yes. Gemelec Electrical Services is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for electrical emergencies across Greater Sydney. We have never missed an after-hours call in 5 years of operation. Call 0498 351 351 any time.

How much does a switchboard upgrade cost in Sydney?

Switchboard upgrade costs vary depending on the size of the property, the number of circuits, and the complexity of the work. Gemelec gives every job a fixed, obligation-free quote before any work starts. Call 0498 351 351 or submit a quote request online.

Does Gemelec install EV chargers in Sydney?

Yes. Gemelec is a certified EV charger installer in Sydney, supporting all major brands including Tesla, Wallbox, Zappi, and Fronius. We handle home and commercial EV charging installations across Greater Sydney.

What areas does Gemelec Electrical Services cover?

Gemelec services all of Greater Sydney, with a focus on the Eastern Suburbs, Inner West, South Sydney, and Sutherland Shire. We are based in Matraville and can reach most Sydney suburbs within 30 to 45 minutes.

Is Gemelec a licensed electrician in NSW?

Yes. Gemelec Electrical Services holds NSW Electrical Contractor Licence 376553C. We also hold NSW Security Master Licence 000108133 for security and CCTV work. All work is fully licensed, insured, and compliant with Australian Standards.
